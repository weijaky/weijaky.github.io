BambooNode 1.0 — Notes that stay on your device

Today I'm shipping BambooNode (竹节笔记) — a notes and file manager for Android built around one simple idea: your notes are yours, full stop.

Why I built it

Most note-taking apps want your account, your cloud, and your attention. I wanted the opposite: a tool that opens instantly, works on a plane, and never phones home. The name comes from how bamboo grows — node by node — which is also how I think notes should be organized: a tree, growing branch by branch.

What's in 1.0

  • Tree-structured notes — organize notebooks and files as nodes within nodes, with tags and full-text search.
  • Markdown editor — tables, task lists, strikethrough, and Mermaid diagrams rendered fully offline.
  • PDF export — turn any note into a polished, shareable document.
  • Git sync — back up your notes to your own Git repository; you own the storage.
  • Fully offline — no account, no sign-in, no network requirement.
  • 19 languages — localized UI and privacy policy for a global audience.

What's next

I'm collecting feedback before planning the next release. If something feels off — or you'd love a specific feature — tell me at weijaky@gmail.com. Every message gets read.

BambooNode is available on Google Play, and the full support page and FAQ live on this site.
