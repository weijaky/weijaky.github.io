About

I've spent 12+ years across the Android stack — Framework, SystemUI, Bluetooth, OTA — fixing what most users never see. That depth now runs under my own apps: fewer bugs, smoother UI, and the discipline to keep them tiny and private.

I write Kotlin, think in Compose and MVI, and sweat the details — smooth 60fps interactions, instant cold starts, and zero network calls you didn't ask for. Everything I build follows one rule: your data belongs to you.

When I'm not coding, I'm usually sketching app icons, writing dev notes for this site, or asking myself the same question before every release: would I install this myself?
