Privacy-first notes & file manager for Android

Google Play Email support

Features

Markdown notes

Clean editor with tables, task lists, and strikethrough support.

Mermaid diagrams

Flowcharts and diagrams rendered right in your notes — fully offline.

Tree organization

Structure notebooks and files the way you think — nodes within nodes, with tags and search.

PDF export

Turn any note into a polished, shareable PDF document.

Git sync

Back up your notes to your own Git repository — you own the storage.

Privacy by design

Data stays on your device. No ads, no accounts — anonymous crash reports only, to fix bugs.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. BambooNode has no servers and does not upload your notes or files anywhere.

Do you collect any personal data?

No. See the privacy policy for the full details.

Can I back up or move my notes to a new phone?

Use the export/backup option in the app, then import the backup file on your new device.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email weijaky@gmail.com with your device model and Android version — I read and reply to every message.

Is there an iOS version?

Not for now. The focus is on making the best possible Android experience first.
